Privacy Policy

Application: ConstructionNewsAI

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Overview

ConstructionNewsAI is a personal-use application that collects and summarizes publicly available construction machinery industry news.

2. Google Account Data

The application requests Gmail permissions to send news digest emails and read email messages for email-related functionality.

3. Use of Data

Gmail data is used only for the application's email delivery and email-related features. It is not used for advertising or marketing.

4. Data Storage

The application stores OAuth authentication credentials locally on the owner's computer. News delivery records may also be stored locally.

5. Third-Party Services

The application uses Google Gmail API for email-related operations. News processing may use external AI services.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored application data and credentials can be deleted by the application owner. Google Account access can also be revoked through Google Account security settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data handling changes.
